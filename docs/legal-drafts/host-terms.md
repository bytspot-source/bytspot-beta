## 1. Who can host paid events
- You must be 18 or older. You must finish **Stripe Connect** onboarding, including identity checks, before paid checkout opens for your event. The API blocks checkout until the connected account can take charges and receive payouts.
- You are the organizer and seller of your event. You are responsible for the venue, permits, capacity, security, insurance and following the law.

## 2. Alcohol
Bytspot doesn't sell, serve or deliver alcohol. If your event involves alcohol, the venue or a licensed provider must handle it, and you must mark the event **21+**. Read the Alcohol Policy (/alcohol-policy). "BYOB for sale", selling bottles through Bytspot, and putting drinks into the pass price are not allowed.

## 3. Money
- Payments go through Stripe. Payouts are sent **after the event**. They are held if the event was cancelled, or if a payment has an open dispute or fraud warning.
- If you cancel, buyers get refunds automatically and you get no payout for those orders.
- If a chargeback is lost, we may recover the amount, including fees, from future payouts. [COUNSEL: confirm the recovery wording.]
- Platform fee: [X]% as shown when you create the event. Taxes: [COUNSEL].

## 4. Location and invites
Your venue's exact address is shown to guests only once they are approved or have bought a pass. Public pages show an approximate area. You can rotate a leaked invite link at any time.

## 5. Enforcement
We may pause sales, cancel events, refund buyers or suspend hosts if there are reports, fraud signals, or breaches of these terms.
