Bytspot

Find the spot. Make the plan. Send the link.

Bytspot is an intent app for people to find spots and plan outings.

Tell Bytspot what you want to do, like a chill date night under $50 near Midtown, and it finds spots that fit and builds a plan you can share with friends.

It reads your intent — vibe, occasion, budget, time, who's coming, and area — and turns that into matching spots and a shared plan. Free on iPhone. Atlanta, starting in Midtown. The day runs from brunch through after hours. Busyness is a typical pattern by hour, not a live crowd count.
